05 / REPRODUCIBLE METHODOLOGY

Research Methods

Equations, sources, measurement limitations and the pathway from this demonstrator to an academic-grade platform.

MODEL 01 / EARLY WARNING

Hazard / Logistic model

P(Cᵢ,ₜ₊ₕ = 1 | Xᵢ,ₜ) = σ(β₀ + βᵀXᵢ,ₜ)

Probability must be trained on historically labeled episodes and assessed out-of-sample. No fitted probabilities are displayed in this MVP.

MODEL 02 / CONDITIONAL COST

Expected systemic loss

ESLᵢ,ₜ = P(Cᵢ,ₜ₊ₕ = 1) × E[Lᵢ,ₜ₊ₕ | C = 1]

Separate event likelihood from the conditional macroeconomic damage; GDP loss units and the horizon must be specified.

MODEL 03 / CONTAGION NETWORKS

Dynamic spillovers

sₜ₊₁ = α + ρWₜʳᵃᵈᵉsₜ + γWᶠⁱⁿsₜ + εₜ

Require observed weighted exposure matrices, stability constraints and independent model validation.

MODEL 04 / EXTREME EVENTS

Tail risk & black swans

ESₐ = E[L | L ≥ VaRₐ]

Stress distributions and conditional tail loss are researchable. The timing of genuinely unanticipated black-swan events is not reliably predictable.
