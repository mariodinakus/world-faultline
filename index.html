05 / REPRODUCIBLE METHODOLOGY

Research Methods

Equations, sources, measurement limitations and the pathway from this demonstrator to an academic-grade platform.

MODEL 01 / EARLY WARNING

Hazard / Logistic model

P(Cᵢ,ₜ₊ₕ = 1 | Xᵢ,ₜ) = σ(β₀ + βᵀXᵢ,ₜ)

Probability must be trained on historically labeled episodes and assessed out-of-sample. No fitted probabilities are displayed in this MVP.

MODEL 02 / CONDITIONAL COST

Expected systemic loss

ESLᵢ,ₜ = P(Cᵢ,ₜ₊ₕ = 1) × E[Lᵢ,ₜ₊ₕ | C = 1]

Separate event likelihood from the conditional macroeconomic damage; GDP loss units and the horizon must be specified.

MODEL 03 / CONTAGION NETWORKS

Dynamic spillovers

sₜ₊₁ = α + ρWₜʳᵃᵈᵉsₜ + γWᶠⁱⁿsₜ + εₜ

Require observed weighted exposure matrices, stability constraints and independent model validation.

MODEL 04 / EXTREME EVENTS

Tail risk & black swans

ESₐ = E[L | L ≥ VaRₐ]

Stress distributions and conditional tail loss are researchable. The timing of genuinely unanticipated black-swan events is not reliably predictable.

MODEL 05 / THREE-DOMAIN SYSTEMIC VULNERABILITY

Economic + Social + Institutional vulnerability

SVIᵢ,ₜ = 0.50 × Eᵢ,ₜ + 0.25 × Sᵢ,ₜ + 0.25 × Iᵢ,ₜ

Every domain D = Σ(wⱼ × gⱼ(xⱼ)) / Σ(available wⱼ), where gⱼ maps each observed indicator to 0–100 pressure. The macro identity Y=C+I+G+(X−M) and the debt dynamics identity inform feature design, but do not determine uniquely justified empirical weights. These provisional weights require estimation or stress testing.

P(Cᵢ,ₜ₊ₕ=1 | Xᵢ,ₜ) = σ(β₀ + βᴱEᵢ,ₜ + βˢSᵢ,ₜ + βᴵIᵢ,ₜ + βᵀZᵢ,ₜ)

This is a proposed research model only: no fitted beta coefficients or calibrated crisis probabilities are published. WGI indicators are perception-based estimates, with statistical uncertainty, and are not evaluations of politicians or political parties.

MODEL 06 / FITTED US RECESSION PROBABILITY (LIVE IN RADAR)

Ridge-logit early-warning model

P(rec in t+1..t+12 | expansion at t) = σ(β₀ + β₁·z(spread) + β₂·z(min₂₄ spread) + β₃·z(NFCI))

Fitted on NBER recession dates 1972–2024 (7 onsets, 560 expansion months) with a ridge penalty λ = 32. Pseudo out-of-sample backtest (expanding window from 1985, labels used only after their 12-month window closed): AUC 0.90, Brier 0.098 vs 0.109 for climatology; leave-one-cycle-out AUC 0.91. The 2022–24 inversion produced a false alarm (out-of-sample peak ≈ 83%) — the model ranks risk well but its top-end probabilities are poorly calibrated. Code: research/fit_us_recession.js, live implementation: radar-core.js.

Stressₜ (% of 2008) = mean_k 100 × (xₖ,ₜ − median₁₉₉₀₋₂₀₁₉ xₖ) / (max₂₀₀₇₋₀₉ xₖ − median xₖ)

k ∈ {NFCI, Baa−10Y spread, VIX, STLFSI} for the US; ECB CISS for the euro area. Global cycle: OECD G20 composite leading indicator plus the share of GDP (17 economies, fixed 2024 weights) whose CLI is below 100 and falling. Global recessions (1975, 1982, 1991, 2009, 2020) coincided with G20 CLI troughs of 96.2, 97.6, 98.8, 95.4 and 89.4; non-recession slowdowns bottomed at 98.5–99.5.
